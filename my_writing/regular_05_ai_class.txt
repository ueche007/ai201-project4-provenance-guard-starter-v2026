I enrolled in this AI engineering course thinking I would learn to create chatbots, but much of my time has gone toward learning to question them. That might sound like criticism, but I don’t mean it that way. Developing the detector taught me how easily a system can produce a convincing score while doing little more than guessing. My initial version labeled a fairy tale as AI-generated. Now I look at what a score really measures before trusting it, and I believe that habit matters more than any individual project.
