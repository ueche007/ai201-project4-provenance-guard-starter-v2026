Our family has a dog called Rocky. He is a brown mixed breed and just turned nine. These days, he spends most of his time sleeping. As a puppy, he raced around the backyard and chased any squirrel he spotted. These days, he takes his time walking and pauses to sniff everything. He still gets thrilled whenever I get home. He greets me at the door, wagging his entire body. My mom calls him spoiled since we feed him scraps from our plates. She has a point. He belongs to our family, and we treat him that way.
