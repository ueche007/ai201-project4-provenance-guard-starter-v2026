I finally saw the movie everyone kept recommending, and I understand the hype, even though I didn’t love it. The opening hour works really well: it’s quick, funny, and the two main actors have believable chemistry. Then the pace drops. A drawn-out stretch in the middle is mostly people gazing through windows, and I caught myself checking my phone a few times. The ending attempts to pull everything together and comes close to succeeding. Would I see it again? Probably not. Would I tell someone to watch it? Surprisingly, yes.
