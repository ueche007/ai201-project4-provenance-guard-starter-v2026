Last summer, a friend and I headed to the coast without much planning, which ended up being both the highlight and the headache of our trip. We spent the first night sleeping in the car since no motel had a vacancy. The next day, we discovered a beach so deserted we felt like we were trespassing. We overspent on seafood, burned in the sun, and lost our way twice heading home because we both questioned the GPS. I’d leave again tomorrow, though I’d reserve a room beforehand.
