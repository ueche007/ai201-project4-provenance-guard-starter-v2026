I keep hearing people call for phone bans in schools, and I see their reasoning, but I doubt a ban solves much. Back in high school, half my classmates used their phones beneath their desks regardless of the rules. The teachers who held our attention weren’t necessarily strict; they taught classes we actually wanted to follow. Banning phones only pushes them into pockets. If schools want students to lift their heads, they have to offer something worth paying attention to.
