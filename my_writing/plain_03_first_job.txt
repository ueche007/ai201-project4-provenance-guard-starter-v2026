My first job was at a local supermarket. I was sixteen then. I packed customers’ bags and collected carts from the parking lot. The wages were low, but I enjoyed earning my own money. My supervisor was demanding. She expected the carts back inside every half hour, even during the hottest summer days. I learned to communicate with customers and keep my cool when people were rude. I stayed at that job for two years. I still shop there, and a few of my former coworkers are still around.
