My neighborhood won’t win any beauty contests, but there’s always something happening. A taco truck pulls up to the corner each evening and somehow never lacks customers. The guy across the road repairs cars in his driveway and offers a diagnosis of yours without waiting to be asked. Kids pedal their bikes down the street until nightfall. During summer, somebody always has music blasting, and no one seems bothered. It’s loud and somewhat untidy, and I’d miss the place if I left.
