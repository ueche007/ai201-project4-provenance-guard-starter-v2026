Each Sunday, my family gets together for dinner at my aunt’s place. She cooks chicken with rice and beans. My cousins join us as well. The younger kids eat first while the grown-ups chat in the kitchen. My uncle always complains that dinner tastes too salty, and my aunt tells him he’s welcome to cook the following week. He never takes her up on it. After we eat, we watch sports on TV or get out the cards. I usually head home around nine since I have class Monday morning. I enjoy these Sundays. They’re the only time all week I get to see everyone together.
