At eight years old, I helped my brother make a couch-cushion fort that stayed up for nearly a week. We kept expanding it with blankets, kitchen chairs, and a flashlight that hardly worked. My mom acted like she hadn’t noticed we’d taken over the living room. We had lunch inside, attempted homework there (without much success), and fought over who claimed the best corner. When we finally took it apart, it wasn’t at her request. One afternoon, we simply got bored, as kids tend to do, and never mentioned it afterward.
