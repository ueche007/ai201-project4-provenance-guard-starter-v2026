I need two buses to reach my job. The first arrives at 6:40. Missing it makes me late, so I head out at 6:30. That trip takes around twenty minutes. Afterward, I wait for my next bus on the corner near the gas station. This bus is generally packed. I usually have to stand. I play music or read something on my phone. On rainy days, the buses move slowly and everybody seems irritated. The whole commute takes roughly an hour. I hope to get a car next year.
